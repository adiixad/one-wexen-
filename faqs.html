THE PRACTICAL DETAILS

Useful answers before you get started.

Every request is different. The details are discussed with you before any work is agreed.

STARTING A CONVERSATION What kind of work can I ask about?

There is no fixed menu on this site. Tell us what is taking your time, what you would like to hand over and what a good result would look like. We can talk through the details and whether it seems like a fit.

STARTING A CONVERSATION Do I need a complete brief?

No. A few sentences about the task, what usually triggers it and what you need from it are enough to start. We can clarify the rest together.

SCOPE & TIMING Can I ask about one-off or ongoing work?

You can bring either kind of request. The possible scope, timing and next step depend on the details, and nothing is agreed until both sides confirm it.

SCOPE & TIMING How do we know if my request is a fit?

We will learn what the work involves, the outcome you expect, the information available and any constraints. Wexen can then tell you whether it appears within its capabilities and what a practical next step might be.

GETTING STARTED What happens after I reach out?

Your inquiry is sent to Wexen through the contact form. Wexen can then follow up to understand the request. Any scope, fees, timing and responsibilities are agreed before work begins.

WORKING TOGETHER How do access and confidential information work?

Only share information or access you are authorised to provide. Agree on the necessary permissions and a suitable sharing method before work starts. Do not include passwords, payment details or sensitive customer information in the public inquiry form.

WORKING TOGETHER How do we communicate and make changes?

Agree on a practical channel and update rhythm for the work. If your priorities or requirements change, discuss them before changing the agreed scope or timeline.

COSTS How is pricing decided?

There is no public rate card here. Charges, payment timing and any additional costs are discussed and agreed before work begins.
