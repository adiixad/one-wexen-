THE FLOW

Four steps, one shared direction.

Each stage builds on the one before it, connecting your context to a clear handoff and a better way to keep the work moving.

WHAT EACH STEP MEANS

Clear from the first conversation.

  1. 01
    SHARE

    Share your workload

    Tell us about your business, the work taking your time and what you would like to change. A short conversation is enough; you do not need a formal brief.

  2. 02
    CLARIFY

    Clarify the goal

    We ask about context, expectations and what a useful outcome looks like. We reflect back our understanding so the goal is clear before planning.

  3. 03
    AGREE

    Agree on the handoff

    Before work starts, agree scope, responsibilities, timing, access permissions, communication and how you will review the work.

  4. 04
    REFINE

    Review and refine

    We take ownership of accepted work, share updates and respond to feedback. Together, we improve the workflow as your priorities evolve.

GOOD WORK STARTS WITH CLARITY

You stay in control throughout.

Delegating a task does not mean handing over your priorities. These shared agreements keep the work clear and client-led.

  1. 01

    Define what done means

    Agree on the intended result and any standards before the handoff begins.

  2. 02

    Keep communication open

    Choose a clear way to share updates, questions and approvals together.

  3. 03

    Share access thoughtfully

    Only provide access you are authorised to share, and agree the permissions needed.
